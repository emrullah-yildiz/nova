## Gap
Nova collects and processes personal data (account email + display name, OIDC
identity, project graphs, AI request metadata, audit events, invite recipient
emails) and ships a working sign-up / Google-login flow, but there is **no
published privacy policy, no terms of service, and no statement of lawful basis
or controller identity** anywhere in the repo or product. A user can create an
account today with no notice of how their data is handled.

## Why it matters (user-rights impact)
GDPR Arts. 13–14 require that, at the point of collection, the data subject is
told the controller's identity, the purposes and lawful basis of processing, the
recipients (sub-processors), retention periods, transfer mechanisms, and how to
exercise their rights. Without this, every account creation is an undisclosed
collection of personal data. This is a foundational gap that several other
tickets (SEC-002 transfers, SEC-003 retention, SEC-004 erasure) depend on.

## Evidence
- `rg -li "privacy policy|terms of service|gdpr|lawful basis|data processing"
  docs README.md` returns **no matches**.
- Working collection paths: `src/enterprise/api-dispatch.mjs` `handleSignup`
  (email + password + displayName), `handleOidcCallback` (Google identity),
  `handleInviteToProject` (invitee email).
- No consent/notice surface in the SPA (`rg -li "privacy|consent" src` finds only
  `src/ai/feedback-client.js`, an unrelated refusal-feedback toggle).

## Recommended action / acceptance criteria
- POLICY/LEGAL (needs counsel): publish a privacy policy and ToS; state the
  controller, purposes, lawful basis (likely contract for account/projects,
  legitimate interest or consent for AI features), retention, sub-processors, and
  the rights-exercise contact/route. Document the EU-transfer mechanism (links to
  SEC-002).
- CODE (follow-up, separate ticket if needed): surface links to both at sign-up
  and in the account/settings UI; require acknowledgement at account creation.
- This ticket is **not** legal advice; it flags the absence of a required notice.
