## 1. Ownership of Your Content
**You own Your Content.** We claim no ownership of the designs, graphs, geometry, or
other outputs you create with Nova. You grant us only the limited, non-exclusive licence
necessary to host, process, display, and back up Your Content **so that we can operate
the service for you** (for example, storing your project in our database, rendering it in
your browser, and syncing it for collaboration). This licence ends when you delete the
content or your account, except for backups that age out on our normal retention cycle.

## 2. Ownership of the Platform
**We own the Platform.** All rights in the Nova software, node library, geometry kernel,

transfer any Platform IP to you beyond the right to use the service per our

## 3. AI features and your data
When you use Nova's optional AI assistant, your prompt and relevant graph context are
sent to a third-party AI provider **you** configure (bring-your-own-key) to generate a
response; this happens directly from your browser to that provider (see
[Data Handling §AI](data-handling.md)). **We do not use Your Content to train AI
models.** Ownership of AI-generated output is yours as between you and Nova, subject to
the AI provider's terms and applicable law; because AI outputs can be unpredictable or
resemble others' works, **you are responsible for reviewing AI output before relying on
or distributing it.**

Those licences govern their respective components; nothing here grants you rights in them
beyond what those licences allow. A list of notable components and licences is available
on request via nova.support@hi-nova.work.

We treat Your Content as confidential and do not access it except as needed to operate,
